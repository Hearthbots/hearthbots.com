Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

The short version: Hearthbots Euchre doesn’t collect any personal data. It’s just you, the bots and the cards.

No personal data

Hearthbots Euchre collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

No network calls

The game code makes no network calls. Unity’s engine reporting (analytics, diagnostics and crash reporting) is turned off.

Your stats stay on your device

Your stats, achievement progress and settings are stored only on your own device. Deleting the app removes them.

Cribbage

Hearthbots Cribbage will follow this same policy when it launches.
