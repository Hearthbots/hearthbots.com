Release Notes

Hearthbots Euchre · newest first

What’s new at the table, one version at a time.

Version 0.7.3 The Lucky Bee release

November 7, 2026

New look

  • Lucky Bee has a new hairdo! Smoother, darker, shoulder-length hair, with the same green bow and plaid. (Look for her in Cribbage, too.)

Fixes and improvements

  • Android: bots and banter now load reliably on Android tablets.
  • Android: fixed the app icon.
  • Updated banter lines.
  • Upgraded to the latest Unity 6 security patch.
  • Turned off Unity's usage reporting for better privacy.

Behind the scenes

  • Avatars, cards, stats, and achievements art now live in a shared Hearthbots library that Euchre and Cribbage both use, so new bot looks show up in both games at once.

Version 0.7.2 The Zeetie release

November 1, 2026

  • Meet Zeetie! Our newest (and most playful) bot. She’s still figuring out this left/right bower thing, so expect a few “Holy Rabbits!” and maybe a “Go Fish!” Pick her with the seat arrows.
  • Everyone sits up taller. All the avatars got their heads lifted, so nobody looks hunched over their cards anymore.
  • Smarter bots. Bots are better about throwing a low card to their partner, and only do it when partner’s trick is really safe.
  • iPad: flip your iPad either way in landscape and the game rotates with it, so the charging cord can stay out of your way.

Version 0.7.1 The Trump Glyphs release

October 31, 2026

  • Trump is easier to spot. Bigger suit glyphs show in the corners of the table once trump is called.
“Been playing it a lot today. Love the larger suit font!” — Trapper, tester

Version 0.7.0 The Achievements release

October 31, 2026

  • Much smaller, so it downloads and installs quicker.
  • New stats and achievements! Check your stats anytime during a game.
  • Drill down into stats for you and your opponents by clicking a name in the stats panel. Tap the < row to go back.
  • Trophy banners pop up when you earn an achievement, or after the hand or match ends. The next deal waits until you’ve seen them.
